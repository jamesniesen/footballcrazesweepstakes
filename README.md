The app handles Sweepstake purchasing,the first 3 following steps:

step1 Upload E-Tickets
Peter Wolf creates the sweepstake tickets for the year.  He sets a number
of tickets aside and creates 'E-Ticket' pdfs that are sent to people who
purchase tickets online through Gems.  He will notify the group and share
a google one drive folder that has the tickets in it.  Download the folder, open
one of the pdfs and review to make sure there are no mistakes and the year is correct.
Copy the files inside the folder  into "..\\net9.0-windows\App_Data\ETicketsToUpload"
Run the app and click on the "Upload E-Tickets" button.  Tom notifies Gems
to turn on the credit card processing for the sweepstakes

setup gems sweepstakes:
        <a href="https://footballcrazesweepstakes.gemsup.co/product/index/purchase-tickets-2024">
            https://footballcrazesweepstakes.gemsup.co/product/index/purchase-tickets-2024
        </a>

Step 2 Test the email service that sends the e-tickets to make sure its working.
Step 3 Upload weekly Purchases from Gems and email (email automatically happens 
when uploaded).
Step 4 Close sales.  Remove link from website.  Tom notifies Gems to turn off
credit card processing.  Right now, the app works only for the current year. Sales
must stop and the final weekly purchase upload finished by Jan 1.  They usually
stop selling around week 8, so you shouldn't have to worry about the year.
